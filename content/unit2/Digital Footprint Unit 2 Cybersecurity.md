**Digital Footprint**  
**Digital footprint** **(can also be called digital shadow or electronic footprint)** is a unique set of your activities on the Internet. It includes your emails, **search history**, videos that you have watched, and comments you have written.  
---

Your **digital footprint** can be used in many different ways. It can be harmful and used against you, and also some of it can be stolen. Although, it also can be helpful. Let’s look at some examples.

The first example is comments on social media.  
Let’s imagine that you leave a hateful statement that hurts someone else. Such an action can lead to a **bad digital shadow**. It can hurt not only your reputation but also the reputation of your friends and family. Such actions on the Internet can hurt your future. Let’s say that 10 years from now you try to get your first job. It was a difficult journey, but you finally found your dream job and things feel great. You arrive at the interview where the hiring manager reviews your digital footprint and sees this hateful comment that you made 10 years ago. For this reason, you are not selected for the job. Not only is this sad or disappointing, but also avoidable.  
---

Now let’s look at another example.  
Let’s again imagine the same scenario. However, in this case, your **electronic footprint** looks different. You arrive at the interview and the hiring manager assesses your **digital footprint.** In this example, they do not see any hateful comments or statements, and you have not posted or been mentioned in anything inappropriate. This time, you end up getting what you wanted.

---

Hopefully, these scenarios make you think about how important your footprint on the Internet and social media is. Please be careful with what you post and what you say because you never know what it will bring upon you in the future. Also, be mindful of people around you, be respectful, and do not get yourself into trouble. 

---

**Key Terms and Facts:**

* **Digital footprint** **can also be called digital shadow or electronic footprint.**  
* **As of January 2024, there are over 5.35 billion internet users worldwide, representing 66.2% of the population.** This means that your digital footprint can be connected to many people all over the world.   
* **Digital footprint:** A unique set of your activities on the Internet, which includes your emails, search history, posts, personal information, text messages, etc.  
* **Search history:** Lists of websites that a user has visited, as well as time of the visit.  
* **Bad digital footprint:** Undesirable online behaviors and activities that can hurt someone or someone’s reputation.  
* **Good digital footprint:** Opposite from **bad digital footprint**; traceable data that builds a positive reputation around you.
