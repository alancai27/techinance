# Techinance Financial Literacy Course Final Quiz

> The one quiz for the whole Financial Literacy course, not a per-unit quiz. 20
> questions, 10 points each. Questions and options are transcribed verbatim,
> including option order, because episode quizzes must match the form exactly.
> See STORY-MODE.md.

Welcome to the only quiz of the Techinance Financial Literacy course! Please
answer all of the questions below to the best of your abilities, and refrain from
the use of unauthorized applications and devices. You may take the test as many
times as you feel suit.

Collected before the questions: Name (First, Last); Which state are you from?
(Country if outside the USA); Email.

**1. What is the 50/30/20 budgeting rule?**

- 50% wants, 30% needs, 20% savings
- **50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings** ✅
- 50% savings, 30% needs, 20% wants
- 50% needs, 30% savings, 20% wants

**2. What is the main strategy to combat impulse buying?**

- Use credit cards only
- Shop without a budget
- **Make a shopping list before going out** ✅
- Purchase everything on sale

**3. Which debt repayment method focuses on paying smallest balances first?**

- Debt avalanche method
- **Debt snowball method** ✅
- Debt consolidation method
- Minimum payment method

**4. What type of loan is a mortgage?**

- Unsecured loan
- Revolving debt
- **Secured loan** ✅
- Personal loan

**5. When you buy a stock, you become a:**

- Company employee
- **Partial owner of the company** ✅
- Company manager
- Financial Deputy Chief

**6. What is a bond?**

- A type of stock
- An ownership share in a company/corporation
- **A loan you give to a company/government** ✅
- A type of mutual fund

**7. At what income level are you required to file taxes?**

- More than $10,000
- **More than $12,200** ✅
- More than $15,000
- More than $20,000

**8. What does ETF stand for?**

- Electronic Trading Fund
- **Exchange-Traded Fund** ✅
- European Trading Fund
- Enhanced Tax Fund

**9. What is the tax filing deadline each year?**

- December 31st
- January 31st
- March 15th
- **April 15th** ✅

**10. Which investment is considered one of the safest with low risk?**

- Individual stocks
- Cryptocurrency
- **Gold** ✅
- Startup companies

**11. What is revolving debt?**

- Debt that disappears after one year
- **Debt you can borrow repeatedly up to a limit** ✅
- Debt that only applies to mortgages
- Debt that has no interest

**12. Which method saves the most money on interest payments?**

- Debt snowball method
- **Debt avalanche method** ✅
- Making only minimum payments
- Debt consolidation always

**13. What is an emergency fund?**

- Money for vacation expenses
- **Money set aside for unexpected expenses** ✅
- Money for regular monthly bills
- Money for entertainment

**14. What are dividends?**

- Taxes you pay on investments
- Fees charged by brokers
- **Portions of company profits paid to shareholders** ✅
- Interest paid on bonds

**15. What is a tax deduction?**

- Money the government owes you
- A penalty for filing late
- **An amount that reduces your taxable income** ✅
- A type of tax credit

**16. Which form do most employees receive for tax filing?**

- 1099
- **W-2** ✅
- W-4
- 1040EZ

**17. What is the main risk of credit card debt?**

- Low interest rates
- Too much available credit
- Monthly statements
- **High interest rates that can create debt cycles** ✅

**18. Why is long-term investing generally recommended?**

- It guarantees profits
- **It allows for compounding returns** ✅
- It has no risk
- It requires no research

**19. What happens when you move to a higher tax bracket?**

- Your entire income is taxed at the higher rate
- **Only the income in that bracket is taxed at the higher rate** ✅
- You pay double taxes
- You get a tax refund

**20. What is the purpose of comparison shopping?**

- To buy the most expensive item
- **To find the best price and quality** ✅
- To shop at only one store
- To avoid using coupons
