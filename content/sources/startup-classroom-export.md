## Module 0 \- Getting Started

Hey, welcome\! Thank you for joining :)

This course is for students who want to learn how to build something. It's very beginner-friendly, you don't need a business background, a co-founder, or any prior experience. Just curiosity and a few hours.

Over the course you'll go through six modules covering everything from finding a problem worth solving to understanding how funding works. Each module has one activity, and by the end you'll have real materials you can use on applications and profiles.

Please fill out the intake form attached to this module to claim two volunteering hours, and remember that once you finish the course, you can submit the certification form to be awarded a certificate\!

Enjoy\!\!\!

\- The Techinance Team
