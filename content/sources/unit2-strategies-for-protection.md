**Strategies for Protection**  
Knowing how to protect your **personal information** is a very important and useful skill that everyone should have. In this lesson, we are going to suggest some steps that will help you to protect your information and minimize your **digital footprint**.   
---

How to protect my **personal information** online?

1) **Use strong passwords** \- use capital letters, symbols, and numbers with a total of 15 minimum. Also make sure that your password does not contain your birthday, address, or name.  
2) **Install the latest updates of your apps and operating systems** \- updates very often have changes that improve the security of the program.   
3) **Do not use the same password**  \- please do not repeat the same password for all of your accounts. This way if one of your accounts gets hacked, the rest of them are going to be secure.   
4) **Use two-factor authentication for your accounts** \- it will enhance the security of your accounts.  
5) **Do not visit suspicious sites** \- some websites can contain **malware** and you will never know.   
6) **Try to not use public Wi-Fi that does not have any password protection** \- **statistics state that 1 in 4 people have experienced a security issue from browsing on unsecured networks.**  
7) **Do not share your personal information over the Internet** \- do not tell anyone your address, phone number, passwords, etc.

---

How to minimize my **digital footprint**?

1) **Limit social media accounts** \- the more social media accounts you are going to have, the bigger your **digital footprint** is going to be.  
2) **Delete old accounts** \- this will help you to minimize your **digital shadow** by simply deleting your old information, photos, etc.  
3) **Update your privacy settings** \- block **third-party cookies** from websites, and turn on enhanced protection on your browser.   
4) **Protect your identity** \- make sure that you do not share your **personal information** and that your information is not posted anywhere on the Internet.   
5) **Ask your friends and family to avoid sharing any photos or videos that might present you in an unfavorable light.**

Please take action to protect your information and minimize your **digital footprint**. Be safe\!  
---

**Key Terms and Facts:**

* **Statistics state that 1 in 4 people have experienced a security issue from browsing on unsecured networks.** This means that every fourth person who uses public networks (WiFi) experiences security problems.  
* **Digital footprint** **can also be called digital shadow or electronic footprint.**  
* **Digital footprint:** A unique set of your activities on the Internet, which includes your emails, search history, posts, personal information, text messages, etc.  
* **Personal information:** Any information that is directly related to you.  
* **Third-party cookies:** Cookies that are placed on websites by third parties (not the owner).  
* **Malware:** A software program that is designed to damage or/and gain unauthorized access to a computer.   
* **Two-factor authentication:** A method of securing your information or account that requires at least two forms of identification.
