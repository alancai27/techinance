About Techinance

Techinance is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We teach technology, neuroscience and finance to young people, for free, anywhere in the world. A lot of our students don't have easy access to this kind of thing, and that's exactly who we built it for.

We cover the stuff that actually comes up in real life: staying safe online, how money and investing really work, and what's going on inside your own head. Good courses shouldn't cost anything, and you shouldn't need someone in your family who already knows this to get started.

Every young person deserves to understand the tools shaping their world, not just use them. So our courses are built to be practical. You won't just learn what a concept means, you'll use it. Whatever your background, we want to hand you the best version of this we can make.
