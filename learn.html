How Story Mode Works

Each unit is an episode. Your decisions change what happens next.

  • Play a role. Read the evidence, ask questions, and decide what to do next.
  • Earn XP. Correct decisions earn points. Wrong ones come with an explanation before you continue.
  • Collect badges. Each badge records a skill you used to finish part of an episode.
  • Pick up where you left off. Your checkpoint is saved on this device.
