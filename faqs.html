Frequently Asked Questions

How long are the courses?

The courses are completely asynchronous, and full flexibility is offered upon the entrance to the courses! However, it is recommended that students take no longer than ninety (90) days to complete a course, as any further time may result in knowledge gaps, and forgetting valuable information.

Enroll in a course →
Who should take these courses?

Our courses are open to everyone – regardless of age or gender, however, we recommend our courses mainly towards 12-16 year olds.

Enroll in a course →
I have registered for a course. Now what?

After completing the form for registration, please wait 2-3 days so we can send you the classroom invite! As soon as you get the classroom invite code, you can join the course and start working on it!

Is Techinance a 501(c)(3)?

Yes! Techinance is a registered 501(c)(3), and provides volunteer hours as well as the PVSA award for volunteers (Note: PVSA is on hold at the moment).
